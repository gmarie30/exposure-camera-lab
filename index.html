YOUR FIRST SHOT

Three dials.
Endless possibilities.

Aperture: A low f-number blurs the background and lets in more light.

Shutter speed: A slow shutter brightens the scene and adds simulated motion trails to your photo.

ISO: A higher ISO brightens the image and adds grain.

Use M for manual control or A for balanced auto settings. Click ISO or Av on the camera, then ▲ / ▼ to adjust. Click inside your photo to move the focus point. These effects reinterpret existing photos; they cannot recover detail or change the original capture settings. Press SPACE to shoot, then download any photo from your roll.
